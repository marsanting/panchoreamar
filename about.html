CUSTOMER SUPPORT SPECIALIST & TICKETING OPERATIONS

The person who actually reads the whole ticket.

I’m Mar, a customer support specialist and virtual assistant from the Philippines. I’ve spent four+ years on support queues — Fintech and eCommerce — handling phone, live chat, email and social channels across the US, the UK and Australian Markets.

In between, I spent two years in ticketing operations as an inventory and fulfilment specialist: pulling tickets across primary and secondary marketplaces, keeping inventory counts accurate, and monitoring market values so pricing stayed in line with what events were actually selling for. It is support work with a spreadsheet attached — and it taught me that a fast reply means nothing if the data behind it is wrong.

Alongside that, I’ve run Shopify stores as a general VA: product research, listing optimisation, website maintenance, and the day-to-day admin that keeps a small business moving. Whatever the queue looks like, my job is the same — understand it, fix it, and write it down clearly for whoever picks it up next.

Answer well. Log it properly. Move on.
